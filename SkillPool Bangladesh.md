## The KSA framework

**K**

### Knowledge

Theoretical understanding, technical knowledge, industry awareness and informed decision-making.

**S**

### Skills

Practical, technical, digital, communication and problem-solving competencies, built through hands-on learning.

**A**

### Attitude

Professionalism, integrity, discipline, teamwork, adaptability, responsibility and a positive workplace mindset.

Knowledge builds understanding. Skills create capability. Attitude drives performance.
