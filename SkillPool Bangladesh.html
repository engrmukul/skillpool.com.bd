Our vision

To become a trusted and impactful organization in skills development, human capital advancement and employment solutions, contributing to a skilled, productive and globally competitive workforce.

Our mission

  • Promote inclusive and industry-responsive skills development.
  • Develop competent human resources through the KSA framework.
  • Connect training with employment, entrepreneurship and income-generating opportunities.
  • Facilitate partnerships with government, the private sector and international development organizations.
  • Support lifelong learning, digital transformation and sustainable livelihoods.

We believe sustainable development begins with skilled, knowledgeable and responsible people. Our approach goes beyond conventional training: by integrating Knowledge, Skills and Attitude, we develop competent, productive and adaptable people who can respond to the changing demands of national and international labour markets.

We bridge the gap between skills and employment through demand-driven training, competency-based learning, professional development, career guidance, job placement and industry-oriented workforce preparation. We also bring together government institutions, private organizations, industries, training providers, development partners and international stakeholders to deliver skills initiatives that make a difference.
